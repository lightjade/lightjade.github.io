Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 6, 2026

Sudoku app values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information collected by the app and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

This app may collect the following information automatically:

  • Advertising Identifier: Advertising ID may be collected to provide advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Device Information: Basic information such as device type and operating system version may be collected for ad optimization.

1.2 Information Stored Locally

The following information is stored only on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers:

  • Game progress (current game state)
  • Game statistics (play time, games completed, records by difficulty, etc.)
  • App settings (theme, board style, sound settings, vibration settings, etc.)

2. Purpose of Using Information

Collected information is used only for the following purposes:

  • Ad Provision: Providing personalized advertisements through Google AdMob
  • App Feature Provision: Saving game progress and providing statistics
  • User Experience Improvement: Maintaining app settings and providing a personalized experience

3. Third-party Services

3.1 Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements. AdMob may collect the following information:

  • Advertising Identifier (Advertising ID)
  • Device and network information
  • Ad interaction information

You can find Google AdMob's privacy policy here.

4. Information Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete all data at any time through the "Reset Data" feature in the app settings.
  • App Deletion: All data stored on the device will be deleted upon app removal.
  • Ads Data: Data collected by Google AdMob is managed according to Google's policies.

5. Privacy Protection

This app protects user privacy in the following ways:

  • Game data is stored only on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers.
  • Does not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.).
  • No account creation or login required.

6. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at the address below.

7. User Rights

Users have the following rights:

  • Right to Data Deletion: All game data can be deleted at any time in the app settings.
  • Right to Opt-out of Personalized Ads: You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.
    • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > Allow Apps to Request to Track (Off)
    • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be modified in accordance with changes in relevant laws and policies. In case of significant changes, we will notify you through an in-app notice.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: tomatobox.apps@gmail.com
